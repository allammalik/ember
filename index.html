EMBER

SMALLBATCH07

Ember is a cold-smoked herbal aperitivo. Juniper and oak over a slow fire, finished with burnt orange peel. Bitter up front, warm at the back. Pour it over ice and take your time.

02 The Pour

Smoke and
Citrus

Sugar would hide it. Ember leads with charred orange and gentian, settles into a resinous middle of juniper and bay, then closes dry and faintly sweet — like the last minute of a fire.

Raised over
slow fire.

03 The Smoke

Juniper
and Oak

Fourteen botanicals, cold-smoked for six hours over fallen oak. No colouring, no syrup, no hurry. What goes in the bottle is what came off the fire.

No shortcuts.
No syrup.

04 The Ritual

Over Ice,
Always

One big cube, a strip of orange peel, nothing else. If the evening runs long, top it with soda — it holds its shape either way.
